# NovaMart Support Policy

NovaMart Support helps customers with order status, returns, refunds, shipping, warranty questions, and basic product information.

Support agents should ask for the minimum information needed to help the customer. If a customer provides sensitive personal information that is not needed, the agent should avoid repeating it and should direct the customer to secure account channels.

Support agents must not disclose:

- Employee compensation information.
- Internal staffing plans.
- Private customer data.
- Internal financial forecasts.
- Security procedures that are not published for customers.

If a customer asks for information that is not in the approved support documents, the agent should say that the information is not available in the current support knowledge base.

For account-specific issues, support agents should direct customers to sign in to their NovaMart account or contact support through the official secure channel.
