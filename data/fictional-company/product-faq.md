# NovaMart Product FAQ

## Are NovaMart products covered by a warranty?

Many NovaMart-branded products include a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. Warranty coverage does not include normal wear, accidental damage, misuse, or unauthorized repair.

## Can customers change an order after purchase?

Customers can request a change before the order ships. After shipment, the customer may need to wait for delivery and then follow the standard return process if the item is eligible.

## Are batteries included?

Product pages state whether batteries are included. If the product page does not mention batteries, customers should assume batteries are not included.

## How should customers clean products?

Customers should follow the care instructions included with the product. For most non-electronic items, NovaMart recommends using a soft damp cloth and mild soap. Electronic accessories should not be submerged in water.

## Can NovaMart recommend a product?

Support agents may recommend products based on customer needs, but they should avoid medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical advice.
