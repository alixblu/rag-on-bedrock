# NovaMart Refund Policy

NovaMart accepts most standard returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in original packaging, and include all accessories that shipped with the order.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method after NovaMart receives and inspects the returned item. Most approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after inspection.

The following items are not eligible for standard return:

- Final sale items.
- Opened personal care products.
- Gift cards.
- Items damaged after delivery because of misuse.

If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, customers should contact NovaMart Support within 7 days of delivery. NovaMart may provide a prepaid return label, replacement, or refund depending on the situation.

Customers can begin a return from their NovaMart account by opening the order, choosing Start return, selecting the item, and following the return instructions.
