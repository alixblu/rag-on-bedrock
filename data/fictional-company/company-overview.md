# NovaMart Company Overview

NovaMart is a fictional online retailer used for Amazon Bedrock retrieval augmented generation labs. The company sells home office supplies, small kitchen tools, everyday electronics accessories, and seasonal household items through a direct-to-consumer ecommerce storefront.

NovaMart's customer support team answers questions about orders, returns, shipping, product care, and account help. The support team should only answer from approved customer-facing policy documents.

NovaMart's public customer promises are:

- Keep return and refund policies clear before purchase.
- Provide practical shipping estimates at checkout.
- Protect customer personal information.
- Avoid sharing internal company information in customer support responses.

This lab dataset intentionally does not include executive biographies, employee compensation, private financial information, unreleased product plans, or internal HR records.
