# NovaMart Shipping Policy

NovaMart ships to residential and business addresses in eligible service areas. Shipping options and prices are shown at checkout before the customer places an order.

Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days after the order leaves the warehouse. Expedited shipping usually takes 1 to 2 business days after shipment when available for the destination.

Orders placed before 2:00 PM local warehouse time are usually prepared the same business day. Orders placed after that time may begin processing on the next business day.

Customers receive a shipment confirmation email with tracking information after the carrier accepts the package.

Shipping delays can occur because of weather, carrier capacity, address issues, or inventory checks. If tracking has not updated for more than 3 business days, customers should contact NovaMart Support with the order number.
